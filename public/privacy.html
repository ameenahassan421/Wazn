Privacy

Wazn — last updated 5 August 2026

Wazn is a strength-training log. It stores what you type into it so it can show it back to you, and it does not sell, rent, or share that with advertisers. This page says exactly what is stored, who processes it, and what never leaves.

What is stored

Your email address To sign you in. Wazn has no passwords — you get a six-digit code by email.
Your training Workouts, sets, weights, reps, RPE, routines, and any notes you write.
A display name and username Only if you set one. Both are optional and blank by default.
Who you follow, and likes Only if you use the Friends tab.

There is no advertising identifier, no location, no health-app integration, no contact upload, and no third-party analytics on your training data.

Your training is private by default

A new account's visibility is Private. Nobody can find you or see anything you have logged until you change that yourself on the Friends tab. If you switch to Followers, people you approve see your finished workouts — name, duration, volume and records. A workout in progress is never visible to anyone else at any setting, and per-set detail is never shown in anyone else's feed.

This is enforced by row-level security in the database, not by the app hiding things. Turning your profile back to Private takes effect on the next read, even for people who already follow you.

Who processes it

Supabase Hosts the database and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the US.
Resend Sends your sign-in code. Receives your email address, nothing else.
Vercel Serves the app itself and collects aggregate page analytics.
OpenRouter Runs the AI model behind Coach's Notes and the routine builder. See below.

What the AI features send — and what they do not

Coach's Notes and the routine builder send a compact block of statistics to a language model through OpenRouter. That block contains numbers and exercise names only: weekly sets per muscle group, estimated one-rep maxes, session counts, and the names of lifts like "Bench Press (Barbell)".

It does not contain your email address, your name, your username, your account id, your workout titles, or any note you have written. That is not a policy someone has to remember — the block is produced by a single database function whose output has no such fields in it.

The model is asked to phrase observations, never to compute them: every figure you see is calculated in the database first. Its output is labelled AI-generated — not medical advice, and nothing it produces is saved to your account until you choose to save it.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your log stays until you remove it. You can delete individual sets from History at any time. To delete your account and everything attached to it, email ameen.hassan421@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and it will be removed — profile, workouts, sets, routines, notes, follows and likes. Deletion cascades in the database, so nothing is left behind in fragments.

This is a beta

Wazn is being tested with a small group. Things will break. If something looks wrong with your data, say so — that is what the test is for.
