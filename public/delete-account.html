Delete your account

Wazn — last updated 23 August 2026

You can delete your Wazn account from inside the app, at any time, without asking anyone. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

From the app

  1. Open Settings (the avatar in the header).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete for good.

The account and everything listed below are removed immediately. There is no waiting period and no recovery window, because there is nothing left to recover from.

If you cannot open the app

Email ameenahassan421@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking for your account to be deleted. You will get confirmation once it is done.

What is deleted

Everything below is erased permanently, not hidden or archived.

Your account Sign-in address, username, password if you set one, and any linked Google or Apple identity.
Your training Every workout, every set, your routines, your personal records and any exercise you created yourself.
Your body log Body weight, measurements, protein days and daily check-ins.
Your coach history Briefings, debriefs, weekly reviews and the record of which ones you were shown.
Your crew Who you follow, who follows you, your invites and anything you liked. You disappear from other people's boards.
Your settings Units, language, theme, weekly target and coach preferences.

What is not deleted, and why

  • The shared exercise catalogue. The built-in movements are not yours and are not removed. Any exercise you created is.
  • Ordinary server logs. Our host keeps short-lived operational logs (request times, error traces) that may contain an account identifier for a few days before rotating out. They are not used to rebuild an account and are not readable as your training.

See also the privacy policy for what Wazn stores while your account is open.
