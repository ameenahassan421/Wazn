Wazn · Design v2.1 — the missing screens

Extends v2 "Loaded Ink, refined" with the four surfaces the launch bundle needs and the handoff never covered: the Coach tab (AI), the Progress dashboard, the Friends tab (feed + leaderboard), and the Finish summary with its shareable card. Every token, radius, motion rule, and voice rule from v2 applies unchanged. The tab bar grows from three items to five.

01

Coach — the AI tab

Two bounded tools, no chat. Coach's Notes reads the lifter's actual numbers back to them with a priority order; the routine builder is a three-question form with one hero action. Amber is earned by priority #1 and the single CTA — nothing else.

Coach's Notes card

Kicker COACH'S NOTES · AS OF AUG 4 + quiet Regenerate (ghost). 3–5 notes: index in 12px mono amber, headline 15/600, support 13/400 --n2, and a data chip — mono 11, amber-300 on 7% tint — quoting the exact numbers the insight came from. The chip is what makes the AI feel like it read the log.

Priority #1 signature

Only the top note carries a 4px vertical knurl band at inline-start. One knurl per screen; severity comes from order, not color.

Routine builder

Dashed amber card (next-up pattern). Three chip rows: goal / days / equipment, single-select, v2 chip states. Generate routine is the screen's one hero button. Result opens as a full-screen preview → Save routine (hero) / Adjust (secondary); saved routines land in the normal Routines list, never auto-activate.

States

Loading: kicker cycles READING YOUR LOG… — no skeleton shimmer. Empty (new user, <3 workouts): "Log 3 workouts and the coach will have something to say." Quota spent: Regenerate goes disabled at 45% opacity, footer says when it resets. Offline/error: amber outlined alert per v2, states what failed and what happens next.

Tab bar

Now five items: Log · History · Progress · Coach (star) · Friends. Active = amber icon+label + 2px top rail. 58–60px unchanged.

This week

Three columns split by inner rules: sessions, volume, streak. Figures 27/600 tnum; streak keeps the v2 plate-bars (4px, heights 7/10/13/16). All three derived, no new tables.

Muscle balance — the signature

One row per group: label 74px, 14px track on --tile1, value right-aligned. The 10–20 target zone is a knurl band at 33–67% of track width (scale caps at 30 sets). Fill: under band = --n5 neutral; in band = amber 500; over = amber 800. Single hue, three meanings — no red, no green, ramp carries the message.

Focus callout

7% amber tint row naming the lagging groups, cross-linking to Coach. This is the "what should I focus on" answer, computed by SQL — the AI only phrases it.

Volume trend

v2 chart grammar exactly: amber 2px line, 8% area, 3.5px last-point dot, solid baseline + dashed 2/4 gridlines. One point per week.

Strength list

Rows: 40px initial tile (or thumb), name + last-session micro, e1RM 20/500 tnum, 4-week delta in mono — ▲ amber, ▼ --n3, → --n5. Row opens the existing exercise detail page. Sorted by most-trained.

Empty state

New user: week card renders zeros, balance chart shows the knurl bands with no fills + "Log a workout to load the bar." Charts never show fake data.

Leaderboard

Card crowned by a 6px knurl band (this screen's one knurl). Rows: rank mono 12, name 14/500, volume 15/500 tnum, sessions micro. Your row sits on 7% amber tint with an amber rank — you always know where you stand. Toggle volume/sessions lives behind the kicker later; ship volume only.

Feed cards

Header: 40px initial tile, name + routine 14/600, timestamp micro; PR badge (knurl overlay) only when a record fell. Stats: three rule-split columns — duration, volume, sets. Fact line quotes the best moment ("new e1RM 142 lb", amber-300). Like: outline heart → filled amber; count in mono. One tap, no comments.

Empty state

No follows yet: leaderboard shows only you + "A leaderboard of one. Invite someone to chase." with Invite as the screen's primary. Invite = share-sheet link.

Privacy

Everything here renders only what RLS already allows: private-by-default, followers see finished workouts only — name, duration, volume, PRs. Never sets-by-set data in the feed.

Share card

Rendered to a 1080×1350 canvas, then native share sheet. Anatomy top→bottom: wordmark (bar + WAZN 600), giant volume figure 56/600 tnum with the session label as a mono kicker, an 8px knurl divider, the three-stat grid, footer: name+date mono left, TRYWAZN.APP in amber-700 right. Pure tokens — exports pixel-identical to the app.

Rules

If a PR fell, the PR replaces volume as the hero number ("156 lb · new bench e1RM"). No profile photos, no map, no emoji. The URL is the only marketing copy — quiet on the plate, legible in a feed.
